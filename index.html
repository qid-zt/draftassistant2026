Every ranking here is your board, untouched: players come off in descending value, with pts/wk breaking ties between equal dollar figures. Nothing is adjusted for position, scarcity, or draft-day market behavior. Gone-by-your-pick odds and the dollar edge on recommendations come from 300 simulated drafts run at every pick, where each simulated team takes near the top of your board with a little randomness. Roster needs are the only thing that shapes what gets recommended to you. Market watch is the one exception, and it is walled off: it runs a separate model that discounts quarterbacks in one-QB leagues, promotes them in superflex, pays a premium for the top two tight ends, and reacts to position runs — purely to flag where real drafters tend to diverge from a value board. It never reorders anything else.
